Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)


How do I schedule a new hauling trip?

Go to the Hauling Schedule module from the sidebar, click the "+ Add Schedule" button, and fill in the driver, vehicle, and destination details.

How to manage yard space capacity?

Navigate to Yard Operations to view the real-time capacity and occupancy status of each yard section.
